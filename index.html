01 / Information We Process

Game Progress

The game stores gameplay information on your device, including selected character class, stages, health, experience, items, upgrades, saved runs, combo data, game settings and reward availability.

Advertising Information

ShatterRealms uses Google AdMob. Depending on your device, region and privacy preferences, Google and its advertising partners may collect or process:

  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Advertising identifiers and app-related identifiers.
  • Ad impressions, clicks and interactions.
  • Device information and diagnostic data.
  • Information used for ad measurement and fraud prevention.

Purchase Information

In-app purchases are handled by Google Play Billing. The game may process product identifiers, purchase status and purchase tokens to provide or restore purchased features.

We do not directly collect your full credit card details through the game.

Contact Information

If you contact us by email, we may process your email address and message to respond to your request.

02 / How Information Is Used

  • To provide gameplay and save your progress.
  • To remember game settings and preferences.
  • To display advertisements.
  • To provide optional rewards after eligible ads.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To prevent invalid advertising activity.
  • To maintain game stability and security.
  • To respond to support and privacy requests.

03 / Advertising

ShatterRealms uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Rewarded Advertisements

Players may voluntarily watch advertisements to receive optional in-game benefits, including:

  • Second Wind — revive after defeat.
  • Fate Reroll — additional item rerolls.
  • Hourly Relic — an optional gameplay reward.

Interstitial Advertisements

Full-screen advertisements may appear during eligible breaks between gameplay stages.

Remove Ads Purchase

The optional "Remove Ads Forever" purchase disables automatic interstitial advertisements. Voluntary rewarded advertisements remain available.

04 / Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request and manage advertising consent.

When applicable, you can review your advertising privacy preferences through the Privacy Options control in the game's settings.

You may also manage advertising preferences through your Android device settings and Google's My Ad Center.
